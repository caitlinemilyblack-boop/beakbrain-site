Eco-Certified Birding Travel

How entries earn their badges

Every listing here carries its evidence. A Certified chip means the company appears on the certifier's own public registry, and the chip links straight to it. A Conservation giving chip means the company publishes a concrete commitment such as a fixed donation per booking or a share of profits, linked to the page that documents it. A Birding chip means the company explicitly offers birdwatching. Companies with a stated love of nature and no published policy appear with no badge at all, so a badge here always means something you can check.

Run an eco-certified birding operation?

This directory grows all year. If you run a certified tour company, a vetted guiding programme or a certified lodge that belongs here, send your certification link and we will review it for the next update.
