Early January
Germany

Stunde der Wintervögel

NABU's hour of winter birds: count at the feeder, garden or balcony for one hour and report what lands. Join via NABU.

January
Asia Pacific and worldwide

International Waterbird Census

Every January, volunteers count waterbirds at wetlands across the world; in Asia and Australasia it runs as the Asian Waterbird Census, coordinated by Wetlands International. Find your national count.

Late January
United Kingdom

Big Garden Birdwatch

The world's largest garden wildlife survey, run by the RSPB. One hour, your garden or balcony, count what lands. Sign up with the RSPB.

Late January
Netherlands

Nationale Tuinvogeltelling

The Dutch garden bird count, run by Vogelbescherming Nederland. Half an hour of counting from your window. Doe mee.

Late January
Belgium

Het Grote Vogelweekend

Flanders counts its garden birds with Natuurpunt the same weekend. A quarter hour and a tally sheet is all it takes. Tel mee.

Late January
Sweden

Vinterfåglar inpå knuten

Sweden's biggest bird count: around 20,000 households report the birds at their winter feeders to BirdLife Sverige. Räkna med.

Mid February
Worldwide

Great Backyard Bird Count

Four days, any country, as little as 15 minutes. Run by Cornell and Audubon, and a perfect first count for a new birder. India joins in force with the Campus Bird Count through Bird Count India. Join the count.

February to June
Europe, Asia and Africa

Spring Alive

Children and families record their first stork, swallow, swift, cuckoo, sand martin and bee-eater of spring with BirdLife; African children track the same birds from September. Record a first sighting.

Late April
Worldwide

City Nature Challenge

Cities compete to record the most wild species in four days, birds very much included, through iNaturalist. Find your city.

May
Worldwide

Global Big Day

The biggest single day of birding on Earth, on World Migratory Bird Day in May. Every checklist counts, from a garden list to a dawn marathon. Read how it works on eBird.

Early May
Germany

Stunde der Gartenvögel

NABU's spring twin to the winter count: an hour for the birds breeding around you, every second weekend of May. Join via NABU.

Late June to early July
New Zealand

NZ Garden Bird Survey

Aotearoa's longest running citizen science project, run by Manaaki Whenua Landcare Research: one hour in the garden, count the highest number of each species. Take part.

First weekend of October
Europe and Central Asia

EuroBirdwatch

BirdLife partners run hundreds of migration watchpoints in one weekend as millions of birds stream south. Find an event.

Late October
Australia

Aussie Bird Count

BirdLife Australia's Bird Week count: 20 minutes in the backyard with the count app, Australia's largest citizen science event. Register to count.

October
Worldwide

October Big Day

Global Big Day's autumn twin, timed for peak migration in October. Details on eBird.

November to April
US and Canada

Project FeederWatch

Count the birds at your feeder through the whole winter, at your own pace. Decades of FeederWatch data track how winter birds are faring. Join FeederWatch.

December to February
Ireland

Irish Garden Bird Survey

Ireland's longest running citizen science survey, with BirdWatch Ireland: a weekly garden tally through the 13 weeks of winter. Take part.

Dec 14 to Jan 5
Americas

Christmas Bird Count

Audubon's count is the longest running citizen science project in the world, in its second century. Join a local count circle and bird with your community. Find a circle.

Year round: eBird · iNaturalist · xeno-canto · BirdMap Africa
