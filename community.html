Birding groups & societies

The bird societies, local clubs and citizen-science projects that cover a place — sorted by country and region, with a plain-language note on each so you know what you're joining. Every direct link here is hand-checked. For total coverage of the smaller clubs we also point you at each country's official, always-current club directory. And because grassroots groups live on Facebook, WhatsApp and Meetup — behind logins no crawler can reach — the list grows by suggestion too. This is the pilot: South Africa and the Netherlands.

How complete is this?

Honestly: the direct links above are a hand-checked start, and the official directories cover essentially every registered club. But the liveliest birding happens in groups that sit behind a login, where no automatic crawler can see or verify them. These are the ones we rely on you to point us to:

  • Facebook groups & pages
  • WhatsApp groups
  • Meetup groups
  • Discord & Telegram servers
  • Regional mailing lists & listservs
  • Members-only club areas
  • University & young-birder clubs
  • Local ringing & atlas teams

Know a group we're missing?

This directory grows with the community. If your local club, society, or Facebook group should be here, send it our way and we'll add it.
