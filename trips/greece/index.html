At a glance

Best months
Late April into early May for Lesvos passage and the mainland breeding season, and November to February for the Dalmatian Pelican flocks at Kerkini and Porto Lagos
Getting around
A hire car on the mainland and on Lesvos. Thessaloniki serves the northern wetlands, Athens the south, and Mytilene the island
The one thing to know
The Evros Delta sits on the Turkish border under military restriction in parts, so access is controlled and wants arranging through the management authority or a local guide

When to Go

The Greek year has two peaks and they are at opposite ends of it. Spring belongs to Lesvos and to the breeding wetlands, and winter belongs to the pelicans, which gather in numbers on the northern lakes.

spring

March to May

The season the country is sold on. Lesvos takes the eastern Mediterranean passage, the vultures are back on the Thracian cliffs and the northern wetlands are breeding.

Go for Lesvos passage and its specialities, the Dadia vultures, the Prespa pelican colony.

Trade off Lesvos accommodation in late April and early May books a long way ahead.

Typical daily average

summer

June to August

Hot and quiet on the mainland, with the vulture hide the one reliable set piece. The Aegean cliffs hold Eleonora's Falcon through the summer.

Go for Vultures at Dadia, Eleonora's Falcon, the breeding heron colonies at Kerkini.

Trade off Forty degrees in Thrace in July and August, and wildfire season is a real constraint.

Typical daily average

autumn

September to October

Autumn passage through the Thracian wetlands and the deltas, with raptors moving south over the same ground.

Go for Wader and raptor passage, the first wildfowl arriving.

Trade off The Greek hunting season opens in autumn and is worth planning around.

Typical daily average

winter

November to February

The pelican season. Kerkini and Porto Lagos hold their largest Dalmatian Pelican flocks, and the birds come into breeding plumage before they leave.

Go for Dalmatian Pelican in numbers, wintering wildfowl, White-tailed Eagle at Kerkini.

Trade off The mountains are snowbound and Lesvos is out of season.

Greece End to End

Athens (ATH), Delphi, Mount Olympus, Prespa, Kerkini, Porto Lagos, Dadia, the Evros Delta

South to north and then east along Thrace, which is the way the country reads. April and May covers the most of it.

Lake Kerkini

A managed reservoir on the Strymonas with a flooded forest at one end and pelicans that come to the boats

The single most photographed birding site in Greece. Dalmatian Pelicans come to the boats at Lithotopos, the flooded forest holds nesting Pygmy Cormorant and heron colonies, and the Mount Kerkini foothills above give Sombre Tit and Rock Nuthatch. Winter brings the large pelican flocks.

Look for: Dalmatian Pelican, Great White Pelican, Pygmy Cormorant, Squacco Heron, Sombre Tit, Western Rock Nuthatch, Spur-winged Lapwing

Also covers: Ano Poroia, Lithotopos, Serres

Also watch for: Water Buffalo are herded on the wet meadows around the lake and are part of how the grazing is managed. European Otter works the flooded forest and Golden Jackal calls from the reedbed at dusk.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to go birding in Greece?

Late April into early May. Lesvos takes the eastern Mediterranean passage in that window, the vultures are back on the Thracian cliffs and the northern wetlands are breeding. Winter is the other answer, for the Dalmatian Pelican flocks at Kerkini.

Is Lesvos a separate trip from the mainland?

It is sold as its own week and works best that way. Skala Kalloni is the base, everything on the island is inside an hour, and the specialities are birds the mainland regions never give you.

Where do I see vultures in Greece?

Dadia, in Thrace. The feeding station hide gives Cinereous Vulture year round, Griffon alongside it, and Egyptian Vulture through the summer. Dadia holds the only breeding Cinereous population left in the Balkans.

What is special about Lake Kerkini?

The Dalmatian Pelicans come to the boats, so it gives the closest view of the species anywhere. The flooded forest holds nesting Pygmy Cormorant and heron colonies, and the winter flocks are the largest of the year.

Do I need permits for the Evros Delta?

The delta sits on the Turkish border and parts of it fall under military restriction, so access is controlled and a local guide or the management authority is the way in. Plan this rather than turning up.

Do I need a car in Greece?

On the mainland, yes. The Thracian wetlands, the Dadia tracks and the Prespa lakes all sit well away from public transport. On Lesvos a car is essential too, and most groups hire at the airport.

What certification should I look for in Greek accommodation?

Green Key, which genuinely leads here. Counting the scheme's own published register gives 652 Greek properties, and the national operator states 670, with over ninety per cent of them hotels or small accommodations.

Who are the birding institutions in Greece?

The Hellenic Ornithological Society is the BirdLife partner and runs the rarities committee. The Society for the Protection of Prespa manages the pelican colony, and the national park authorities run Dadia and the delta reserves.
